# Content and Microcopy Guide

## Voice

Short, calm, factual and supportive. Avoid patronizing, diagnostic or over-celebratory language.

## Evidence language

Prefer: “You recalled this without a hint”, “More evidence needed”, “Due for review”, “You practiced…”. Avoid: “You have mastered 83%”, “You are bad at listening”, “AI detected you are losing motivation”, unless a later validated product explicitly supports such claim and governance approves the wording.

## Practice vs Check

Practice: “Try it”, “Need a hint?”, “Try once more.”  
Check: “Check — see what you remember.” No pre-submit hint.

## Retry

After wrong first response: “Not quite. Here’s why… Try once more.” After successful retry: “Nice recovery. We’ll keep reviewing this.” Do not say “Correct on your first try.”

## Skip

“Skip for now” / “We’ll come back to this later.” Never shame; never award completion simply for skipping.

## Recommendation reason examples

- Due for review.
- Continue your current objective.
- Review a prerequisite first.
- Practice a different example after a recent error.
- Try the same idea in a different format.
- Optional challenge.

## Offline/sync

- Offline: “You’re offline. Available lessons still work.”
- Queued: “Saved on this device. Waiting to sync.”
- Synced: “Synced.”
- Failure: “We couldn’t sync yet. Your saved work is still on this device.”

Never say “Progress saved to your account” before server acceptance is known.

## Content staff

Use explicit revision language: “Publish new revision”, “Published revisions can’t be edited”, “Create a new draft from this revision.” License blockers must say exactly what is missing.
