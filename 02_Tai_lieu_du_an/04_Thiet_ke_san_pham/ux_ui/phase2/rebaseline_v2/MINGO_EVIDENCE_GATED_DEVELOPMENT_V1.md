## Dual track: neither certifies the other

**Track A — Are we building the right product?** Investigate the problem/circumstance, understandability, meaningful learning and repeated usefulness. Source: actual learner/owner/content research and appropriately designed outcome evidence. A theory, sample fixture, many clicks or user liking alone does not satisfy this track.

**Track B — Are we building the product right?** Verify requirements, contracts, behavior, accessibility, responsive layout, rendering, performance, reliability, security and operations under declared environments. Source: executable checks, adversarial verification, measured runtime/device evidence and independent review. Technical correctness does not establish product usefulness, learning or human design acceptance.

## Five validation layers

| Layer | Evidence to collect | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **1 Behavior** | Requirement-derived route/state/action and adversarial tests; retain expected/forbidden outcomes | Tested fixture behavior does not implement a domain service or establish ease/learning |
| **2 Semantics/accessibility** | Labels/order, non-color states, 48dp, contrast, 200% text, reduced motion, keyboard/focus; actual TalkBack journey | Automation supports only its assertions; real spoken traversal/device behavior requires a human |
| **3 Responsiveness** | Core/recovery surfaces at 360/common/larger Android widths and 1.0/1.3/1.5/2.0 text, long Vietnamese copy, light/dark where supported | No guarantee for every unsupported device, locale or scrolled focus state |
| **4 Visual regression** | Recorded technical candidate approval, justified golden refresh, exact source/asset/build provenance | Matching a new baseline does not approve visual identity or art direction |
| **5 Human product/art direction** | Product Owner visual/product review, content review, real usability/accessibility, independent QA | Liking the candidate does not prove contracts/reliability; actual identities/date/evidence needed |
