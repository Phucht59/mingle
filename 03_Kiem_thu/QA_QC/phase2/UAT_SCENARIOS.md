# Product / UAT Scenarios for Phase 2

These are owner/product acceptance scenarios for the UX system, not a real-user efficacy study.

1. **New learner skips placement** and reaches Home without being blocked or told a fake level.
2. **Learner completes one finite cycle** and can stop cleanly instead of being pushed into infinite content.
3. **Learner answers wrong then retries correctly** and sees supportive feedback without erasing the first error semantics.
4. **Learner uses a hint** and the product does not treat the result as unaided.
5. **Learner skips** and sees no fake completion/reward/mastery.
6. **Learner goes offline**, completes available work, and understands device-saved vs synced.
7. **Learner opens Profile** and can understand progress/evidence without a fake precision score.
8. **Learner previews a locked objective** and understands the prerequisite without unlocking it.
9. **Recommendation service is unavailable** and the learner still has a usable path.
10. **Content staff publishes a revision** and clearly understands published content is immutable.
11. **Reviewer encounters missing license** and cannot proceed to publish.
12. **Staff opens learner detail** and sees evidence/uncertainty rather than diagnosis.
13. **Staff visits intervention/analytics/admin shells** and can tell they are future-capability surfaces, not fabricated production data.
