8. LEARNING EXPERIENCE OPTIMIZATION LAYER
=====================================================================

Formalize a conceptual learner state with three separate areas.

--------------------------------------------------
A. KNOWLEDGE STATE
--------------------------------------------------

Signals may include:

- correctness;
- unaided success;
- assisted success;
- repeated retrieval;
- delayed retrieval;
- transfer;
- misconception;
- item/context coverage.

--------------------------------------------------
B. LEARNING EXPERIENCE STATE
--------------------------------------------------

Signals may include:

- response latency;
- repeated error;
- hint;
- skip;
- replay;
- abandonment;
- retry;
- resume;
- voluntary next session;
- optional self-report.

DO NOT infer:

“bored”
“lazy”
“unmotivated”
“bad learner”

from a single signal.

--------------------------------------------------
C. CONTEXT STATE
--------------------------------------------------

Examples:

- session time;
- connectivity;
- offline availability;
- device capability;
- accessibility settings;
- content availability;
- interruption/resume context.

These models are conceptually separate.

Do not create premature production ML.
