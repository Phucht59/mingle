# Pilot and Content Constraints

## Content sourcing

The project expects substantial use of legitimate public/open learning sources because the initial team does not have the resources of a dedicated curriculum organization.

This creates product requirements around:

- attribution/licensing review;
- source provenance;
- content versioning;
- editorial quality control;
- safe transformation/adaptation of source material;
- separation between source content and product-specific learning objects.

## Pilot scale

An early pilot size of approximately **5 users** has been discussed as a realistic resource-constrained starting point.

A pilot of this size is useful for:

- usability defects;
- flow breakdowns;
- offline/sync bugs;
- qualitative learner reactions;
- instrumentation validation.

It is not sufficient for robust production ML evaluation or broad causal claims about pedagogy.
